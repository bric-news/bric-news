detail: "Licking County issued a disaster declaration on August 27 after severe flooding in August damaged homes, roads, and property across Pataskala, Heath, Hebron, Buckeye Lake, and parts of Newark, with Pataskala the most severely affected area. Emergency management staff are conducting damage assessments and organizing resource centers for affected residents. County Commissioner Tim Bubb said the declaration is meant to position residents to receive financial assistance for recovery, noting that while not all of the county's 185,000 residents suffered severe damage, enough did to warrant the request. On September 2, Governor Mike DeWine requested federal disaster relief assistance for 23 Ohio counties, including Licking and Franklin, asking for activation of an individual assistance grant program to help cover temporary housing, home repairs, rental assistance, property replacement, counseling, and unemployment assistance costs. DeWine also asked the federal government to make Small Business Administration low-interest loans available to cover losses not covered by insurance. Recovery and cleanup efforts were underway as of early September."
