detail: "As road improvements leading into Upper Arlington's Five Points intersection wrap up this fall, including a final phase of work on Northwest Boulevard, the city has spent the past year developing and refining concepts for redesigning the intersection itself, which has struggled to handle traffic safely and efficiently. During community outreach earlier this year, about 120 residents attended a public meeting and 1,475 people completed a follow-up survey. Respondents favored installing a roundabout, provided the final design addresses pedestrian and bicycle safety, potential effects on nearby businesses, and efficient access for emergency vehicles. The city will present refined design concepts to City Council at its September 14 meeting, 6 p.m. at the Municipal Services Center on Tremont Road, after which a recording and the presentation will be posted to the project's page on the city's website. Officials expect the project to move into detailed design in 2027, with construction targeted for 2028."
