summary: "NBC4's analysis of Franklin and Delaware county auditor records found the top single-family home sales in August 2026 ranged from about $1.8 million to $2.5 million. Franklin County's priciest sale was a $2.4 million home in Upper Arlington, which also had the second-highest sale at $2.3 million. Delaware County's top sale was a $2.5 million home in Dublin, with three of its five highest sales located in the city of Delaware. The analysis excluded apartments, duplexes, and unrelated land purchases."
detail: "NBC4 reviewed August 2026 property sale records from the Franklin and Delaware county auditors' offices, limiting its analysis to single-family dwellings and excluding apartment, duplex, and unrelated land sales. In Franklin County, the top sale was a home at 5000 Slate Run Woods Court in Upper Arlington at $2.4 million, followed by another Upper Arlington home at 2292 Dorset Road at $2.3 million. Rounding out the top five were two New Albany homes, at $2.222 million and $2.1 million, and a Bexley home at $2.185 million.\n\nIn Delaware County, the top sale was a Dublin home at 5500 Muirfield Court, which sold for $2.5 million, the highest figure in either county. The city of Delaware itself accounted for three of the county's five highest sales, ranging from about $1.84 million to just under $2 million. A Johnstown home on Robins Road rounded out the list at $1.795 million.\n\nThe list offers a snapshot of where luxury demand concentrated in the Columbus area last month, with Upper Arlington, New Albany, and Dublin's Muirfield area again showing up as the highest-value pockets in their respective counties."
