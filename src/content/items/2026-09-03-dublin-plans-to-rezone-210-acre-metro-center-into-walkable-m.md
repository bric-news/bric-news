summary: "Dublin officials are advancing plans to redevelop the roughly 210-acre Metro Center area, currently a suburban office park with about 90 acres of surface parking, into a mixed-use district with housing, retail, and public space. The city's Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted in December 2024, calls for new zoning and design standards to guide the change. A Planning and Zoning Commission work session is set for September 17, with a commission recommendation expected in fall 2026 and Dublin City Council considering rezoning in winter 2026-27."
detail: "Dublin is moving forward on a long-planned overhaul of Metro Center, a roughly 210-acre suburban office park where more than 90 acres, about 7,700 parking spaces, are currently surface parking and asphalt. The city's Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted in December 2024, envisions the area becoming a walkable, mixed-use, sustainable district that still functions as a major employment center, with new housing, retail, restaurants, and public gathering space layered into the existing office campus.\n\nCity planners are now drafting the zoning and design standards needed to implement that plan. A Planning and Zoning Commission work session is scheduled for September 17, followed by a commission recommendation expected in fall 2026. Dublin City Council would then take up the proposed rezoning in winter 2026-27, with community input continuing throughout.\n\nOne focus area is the Frantz Road Gateway, intended to link Metro Center to Dublin's existing trail network and surrounding neighborhoods with a connected trail corridor and linear park space. City planner Chris Will said the goal is to reinvest in an already-developed area rather than expand Dublin's footprint outward, describing it as a different model than the city's Bridge Park district rather than a copy of it."
