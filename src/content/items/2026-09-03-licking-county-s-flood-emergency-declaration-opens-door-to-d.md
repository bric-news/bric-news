title: "Licking County's flood emergency declaration opens door to disaster aid for owners"
summary: "Licking County declared a state of emergency after August flooding damaged homes, roads, and property in Pataskala, Heath, Hebron, Buckeye Lake, and parts of Newark, positioning the county to receive disaster aid. Governor Mike DeWine has requested federal disaster relief for 23 counties, including Licking and Franklin, that would fund individual assistance for temporary housing, home repairs, rental assistance, and property replacement, plus SBA low-interest loans for uninsured losses."
detail: "Licking County issued a disaster declaration on August 27 after severe flooding in August damaged homes, roads, and property across Pataskala, Heath, Hebron, Buckeye Lake, and parts of Newark, with Pataskala the hardest hit. The South Fork of the Licking River and the Licking River reached major and moderate flood stages after heavy rainfall, submerging portions of a KOA campground and Interstate 70 near Buckeye Lake and prompting several water rescues.\n\nOn September 2, Governor Mike DeWine requested federal disaster relief for 23 Ohio counties, including Licking and Franklin, saying the storms exceeded the state and local governments' capacity to respond. The request asks the federal government to activate an individual assistance grant program covering temporary housing, home repairs, rental assistance, property replacement, and other recovery costs, along with Small Business Administration low-interest loans to cover losses that were not insured. Statewide preliminary damage assessments found more than 50 homes and businesses destroyed, more than 800 with major damage, and more than 475 with minor damage.\n\nLocally, county emergency management staff are still completing damage assessments and have begun hosting multi-agency resource events, the first in Pataskala on September 3, with more planned in Hebron, Buckeye Lake, and Newark. Commissioner Tim Bubb said the declaration is meant to help residents recover and repair their homes."
why_it_matters: "A landlord with flood-damaged property in Pataskala, Hebron, Buckeye Lake, or Newark should watch for the federal individual assistance and SBA low-interest loan programs Governor DeWine has requested, since those would cover repair and uninsured-loss costs the county's own declaration cannot pay for directly."
