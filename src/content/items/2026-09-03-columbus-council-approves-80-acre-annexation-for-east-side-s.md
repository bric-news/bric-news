detail: "Columbus City Council passed the second reading of an ordinance 8-0 on Tuesday, approving a request from Abundant Home Solutions LLC to annex and rezone about 80 acres on the north side of Sawin Drive, roughly 1,300 feet west of Talley Road, on the city's east side. Councilman Frank Miller abstained on both the first and second readings because he lives nearby in the Villas of Stonecrest. The rezoning changes the land's designation from Agriculture: Preferred to Residential: Two-Family. Developer Daryl Hodgson told council the plan is to build owner-occupied twin-patio homes for residents 55 and older, similar to the adjacent Villas of Stonecrest retirement community, along with a section of single-family homes, targeting prices between $300,000 and $450,000. Hodgson pointed to the city's 2024 housing study, which identified a need to build about 300 new housing units a year through 2035 across price points, including more homes priced above $300,000 and more 55-plus communities; city officials said Columbus is currently on pace to meet that annual target. Planning officials noted the site is unusual for its size and location outside the floodway or floodplain, as the city has limited land available for new housing due to flood constraints elsewhere."
why_it_matters: "The rezoning adds a specific, near-term supply of $300,000 to $450,000 owner-occupied homes and a 55-plus community on the east side, giving investors a concrete price point and buyer profile to watch as the project moves toward construction."
