summary: "The Ohio Supreme Court ruled unanimously on September 1 that the city of Trenton must place a proposed charter amendment restricting large-scale data centers before voters in a special election. The amendment would bar construction of data centers requiring more than 25 megawatts within city limits. The ruling would not stop a nearly 900,000-square-foot, four-building Amazon Web Services campus already under construction on 141 acres in Trenton, but it could affect a larger, planned AWS project of 18 buildings on about 600 acres in neighboring Madison and St. Clair townships."
detail: "The Ohio Supreme Court ordered the city of Trenton, near Cincinnati, to let voters decide a charter amendment that would prohibit construction of data centers requiring more than 25 megawatts of power within city limits. The unanimous ruling, issued Tuesday, September 1, sided with residents who had circulated a petition and submitted it to Trenton City Council in July after gathering signatures from more than 10% of the city's registered voters, the threshold the city charter requires for a ballot measure. Trenton is already home to a nearly 900,000-square-foot, four-building Amazon Web Services data center campus under construction on 141 acres; that project would not be affected if the ban passes, since it is already underway. A second, larger AWS project, with 18 buildings planned across about 600 acres in Madison and St. Clair townships, is also in the works. This was the second data center ballot case the Ohio Supreme Court has ruled on in the past month, following a similar fight in Wilmington, Ohio. The ruling affirms that Ohio municipalities cannot block a validly petitioned charter amendment from reaching the ballot, regardless of the underlying zoning or land use dispute."
why_it_matters: "The ruling confirms that Ohio residents who gather enough valid signatures can force a public vote on local land use questions even when a city council or developer objects, a precedent Columbus-area investors should watch if a citizen group ever petitions to restrict development near their own holdings."
