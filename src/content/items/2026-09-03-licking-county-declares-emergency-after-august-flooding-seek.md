detail: "Licking County commissioners declared a state of emergency on August 27 after flooding in mid-to-late August damaged homes, roads and property across Pataskala, Heath, Hebron, Buckeye Lake and parts of Newark, with Pataskala the most severely affected. The South Fork of the Licking River and the Licking River reached major and moderate flood stages after heavy rain, submerging portions of the KOA campground and Interstate 70 near Buckeye Lake and prompting several water rescues. On September 2, Governor Mike DeWine asked the federal government for disaster relief covering 23 counties, including Licking and Franklin, requesting activation of an individual assistance grant program to help cover temporary housing, home repairs, rental assistance, property replacement and other recovery costs, along with Small Business Administration low-interest loans for uninsured losses. Statewide preliminary damage assessments found more than 50 homes and businesses destroyed, more than 800 with major damage and more than 475 with minor damage. Licking County's emergency management agency has held a multi-agency resource event in Pataskala and plans similar events in Hebron, Buckeye Lake and Newark, with local businesses and nonprofits providing lodging, meals and cleanup supplies to affected residents."
why_it_matters: "If the federal disaster declaration comes through, landlords with damaged rental units in Pataskala, Hebron, Buckeye Lake or Newark could qualify for SBA low-interest loans and rental assistance funds that private insurance alone would not cover."
