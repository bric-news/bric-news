detail: "Dublin officials are working to transform Metro Center from a suburban office park dominated by parking lots into a walkable, mixed-use district with housing, retail, restaurants, public space, and stronger connections to the city's trail network. The proposed redevelopment covers about 210 acres, more than 90 of which are currently parking lots and asphalt, representing roughly 7,700 parking spaces. The plan builds on Dublin's Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted in December 2024, which calls for the area to become a walkable, sustainable, mixed-use district while remaining an important employment center. The plan includes proposed zoning changes and design guidelines meant to create a stronger sense of place, improve pedestrian connections, and add green space and public areas. The proposed district would mix commercial, residential, and civic uses, with connections to surrounding neighborhoods. One focus area is the Frantz Road Gateway, which calls for a connected trail corridor, linear park space, and a transition zone between Metro Center and nearby neighborhoods. The plan remains in an early phase, with zoning changes and design guidelines still to be finalized before specific development projects can move forward."
