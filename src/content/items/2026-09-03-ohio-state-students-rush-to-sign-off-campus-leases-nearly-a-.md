summary: "Students renting near Ohio State's campus described signing leases for the following school year as much as eight months before move-in, driven by fear that houses near campus will fill up. Students said they felt pressured to commit before seeing all their options, then had to backfill lease spots when roommates dropped out over the following months. Ohio State's Off-Campus and Commuter Student Engagement office pointed students to its housing network, which includes a property company survey covering business practices, as a way to vet landlords before committing to an early lease."
detail: "Students in Ohio State's university district described committing to leases for the 2026-2027 or 2027-2028 school year as early as December, months before they are set to move in, saying they feared losing a house to another group of renters if they waited. One third-year student said she signed in March after searching since December, then spent months finding replacements as roommates dropped out of the lease. A university housing official said the timeline varies by student, with some searching in fall and others waiting until spring, and pointed students to Ohio State's off-campus housing network, which connects the university, Student Legal Services, property owners and city departments. The network includes an annual survey landlords and property companies complete describing their business practices, which the university says gives prospective tenants a way to research a landlord before signing. The early-signing pattern shapes leasing timelines for property owners and managers operating near campus, who see turnover decisions and renewal offers made far ahead of the following fall's move-in date."
