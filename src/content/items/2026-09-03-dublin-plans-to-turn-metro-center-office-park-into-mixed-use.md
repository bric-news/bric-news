title: "Dublin Plans to Turn Metro Center Office Park Into Mixed-Use District"
summary: "Dublin officials are advancing a plan to redevelop the roughly 210 acre Metro Center area, currently a suburban office park with about 7,700 surface parking spaces, into a walkable district with housing, retail, restaurants and expanded trail connections. The city's Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted in December 2024, calls for new zoning and design standards rather than a full replica of Dublin's Bridge Park district. A Planning and Zoning Commission work session is set for Sept. 17, with council expected to consider rezoning in winter 2026-27."
detail: "Dublin's Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted in December 2024, aims to transform roughly 210 acres of suburban office park, more than 90 acres of which is currently parking and asphalt representing about 7,700 spaces, into a walkable, mixed-use district. The plan calls for a mix of commercial, residential and civic uses, along with proposed zoning changes and design guidelines meant to create stronger pedestrian connections, more green space and links to Dublin's existing trail network.\n\nCity planners say the redevelopment is not intended to replicate Dublin's Bridge Park district, but to apply similar lessons about connectivity and reinvesting in already-developed land rather than continuing to build outward. One focus area, the Frantz Road Gateway, calls for a connected trail corridor and a linear park linking Metro Center to nearby neighborhoods.\n\nThe city is currently drafting the zoning and design standards for the plan area. A Planning and Zoning Commission work session is scheduled for Sept. 17, followed by commission review and a recommendation this fall. Dublin City Council is expected to take up the proposed rezoning in winter 2026-27, with community input continuing through the process."
