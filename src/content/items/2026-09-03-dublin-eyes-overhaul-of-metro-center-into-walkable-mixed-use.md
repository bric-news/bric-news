detail: "Dublin is advancing a plan to convert its Metro Center office park, roughly 210 acres dominated by parking lots and asphalt including more than 90 acres and about 7,700 spaces, into a mixed-use district with housing, retail, restaurants, civic space and stronger trail connections. The redevelopment stems from the city's Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted in December 2024, which calls for new zoning and design guidelines to create a walkable district while keeping the area an employment center. One focus area is the Frantz Road Gateway, envisioned with a connected trail corridor and linear park linking Metro Center to nearby neighborhoods. City planners say the goal is not to replicate Dublin's Bridge Park district but to bring similar connectivity and reinvestment to already-developed land rather than expanding the city outward. The Planning and Zoning Commission will hold a work session on September 17, with commission review and a recommendation expected this fall; Dublin City Council is expected to take up the proposed rezoning in winter 2026-27, with public input continuing throughout the process."
