detail: "The city of Newark is studying a property in Morgan Township, in southern Knox County near Utica, as a possible future water source, Water Administrator Brandon Fox told The Reporting Project. Fox said the city continually evaluates long-term water sources and that the Knox County property could add to Newark's supply, but stressed there is no immediate need since the city currently has plenty of water. If the site proves viable as a well field, reservoir, or both, the city would treat it as a regional asset that could benefit Utica and townships between Knox County and Newark, not just Newark itself. A reservoir on the land could release water into the North Fork of the Licking River, from which Newark already draws water farther downstream at its treatment plant on Waterworks Road; a well field could feed the river directly or be piped straight to the plant. The city's information sheet on the proposal also notes potential for community recreational use on the site, such as a park or walking path. Fox said the process will involve years of study and funding decisions, and that the city intends to coordinate with Utica officials and township trustees to avoid negative effects on the surrounding area."
why_it_matters: "A viable regional water source would support future development capacity across Newark, Utica, and nearby Knox County townships, though Fox's own timeline of years of study means it is not a near-term factor for site selection."
