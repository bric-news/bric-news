detail: "Realtor.com's July 2026 rent report put the median asking rent in the Columbus metro area at $1,181, down 1.7 percent from a year earlier and roughly flat compared to January's $1,187. The decline mirrors a national pattern now in its third straight year: the median asking rent across the 50 largest U.S. metros was $1,695 in July, down 1.4 percent year over year, though still 15.3 percent above pre-pandemic levels. Realtor.com attributes the multi-year softening largely to increased multifamily construction, which has added supply in many metros. The report also compared renting to buying in Columbus, estimating the monthly cost of buying a starter home at $2,111, based on a 10 percent down payment, prevailing 30-year mortgage rates, and average taxes, insurance and HOA fees, against $1,181 to rent a similarly sized unit, a savings of $930 a month, or about 79 percent cheaper to rent than buy. Renting was cheaper than buying in all 50 major metros the report tracked. Seven other metros, not including Columbus, showed signs of shifting toward buyers as home prices fell faster than rents."
