detail: "Realtor.com's July 2026 rent report puts the Columbus metro's median asking rent at $1,181, a 1.7 percent decline from a year earlier. That is a shift from January 2026, when Columbus rent was $1,187, essentially flat year over year. The report covers 0 to 2 bedroom units across the 50 largest US metro areas, and Realtor.com says the national pattern now shows three consecutive years of year-over-year rent declines, with the median across those 50 metros at $1,695 in July, down 1.4 percent from a year earlier and attributed mainly to increased multifamily construction expanding the supply of available units.\n\nThe report also compares renting to buying in Columbus. Realtor.com estimates the monthly cost of buying a starter home in the metro at $2,111, based on a 10 percent down payment, prevailing 30 year fixed mortgage rates, and average taxes, insurance and HOA fees, against the $1,181 median rent, a gap of $930 a month, or renting running about 79 percent cheaper than buying. Nationally, renting saves an average of $858 a month over buying in all 50 major metros the report covers. Realtor.com's economists note that while renting is cheaper today in most markets, buying conditions are improving relative to renting in a handful of metros, though Columbus is not named among them."
